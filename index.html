Intro

I am

Yo! I'm Dipansu - a developer, a learner, and an engineer navigating the space between logic and meaning.

This site is where I write, build, and think out loud. You'll find notes on technology and systems, reflections on philosophy and stoic thought, and occasional dives into personal finance - three subjects that, on the surface, seem unrelated, but to me are all part of the same pursuit: understanding how things work, whether they're machines, minds, or markets.

I started this space not to have all the answers, but to document the process of searching for them. Feel free to explore my work, read my thoughts, or just say hello..

About

I'm someone who finds equal joy in debugging a stubborn piece of code and When I read a page of Marcus Aurelius once again, I think that good engineering and good philosophy share the same root: discipline, patience, and a refusal To take things on their own merits.

During the day I work as a Software Engineer specializing in Embedded Linux and Cybersecurity in MedTech field. I'm usually reading, writing, or dropping down at night. a rabbit hole about Stoicism, mental models, or how compound interest quietly reshapes a life.

This blog is my way of connecting those dots publicly : imperfect thoughts, Here are some works that are still in progress, and from time to time I come across an insight that I consider worthwhile sharing. When I'm not working or writing, you'll probably find me chasing the next idea worth obsessing over.
